This repo is to showcase the work behind the story https://nyunews.com/news/2024/10/25/annual-campus-safety-report/. I also visualized this story for personal use - please kindly refer to the html document "campusp-crime-stats"

Some files are too large to be put on GitHub, but they are available through NYC Open Data and the NYU Department of Campus Security.

A friendly heads-up: the NYPD seems to have changed its CRS system in 2020, which is the reason why I only compared stats from 2021-23 in the latter half of the document. The department didn't reply to my inquiry.
